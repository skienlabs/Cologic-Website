Services

Four ways we move your product without losing the chain.

From inter-city transport to nationwide rail reach and secure warehousing, every Cologic service is built around one non-negotiable: cold-chain integrity, maintained across every leg of the journey.

01

Primary Transport

Inter-city stock movement

  • Reliable, scheduled road transport between major cities
  • Dedicated & ad-hoc vehicles flexed to delivery volumes
  • Cold-chain integrity maintained across every leg of the journey
02

Secondary Transport

Intra-city, last-mile delivery

  • Built for retail, modern trade, e-commerce, quick commerce, cloud kitchens, QSR & B2B
  • Every drop follows strict SOP-driven handling — zero temperature compromise

  • Careful handling for e-commerce & quick-commerce, tier 1 & 2 cities
