About Us

Built around the cold chain — not around it as an afterthought.

Cologic brings hands-on experience of what it actually takes to run a cold chain — present across six major cities today, and growing: Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, Bangalore and Delhi. Our infrastructure is built specifically around temperature-sensitive goods: dedicated cold storage, reefer-equipped delivery fleets, and a team that treats on-time and on-temperature as the same requirement.

We see ourselves as a growth partner — reaching every consumer touchpoint your brand needs, from city-to-city movement to last-mile delivery for retail and e-commerce outlets, to secure warehousing with live inventory visibility. We have experience handling −18°C to +4°C, across every temperature-sensitive product category, to spec, every time.

Mission

To keep every temperature-sensitive product exactly as it should be — from origin to doorstep — through dedicated cold-chain infrastructure, real-time visibility, and teams on the ground in every city we serve.

Vision

To be India's most trusted name in temperature-controlled logistics — the cold chain brands turn to first, in every city that matters to them.
